About MOVEO

MOVEO is a startup concept developed to make fitness activities and local event discovery more convenient. It aims to bring activity tracking and fitness event information together in one platform.

Our Mission

To provide an accessible platform where users can monitor their fitness activities and discover local fitness events easily.

Our Vision

To become a platform that encourages active lifestyles and connects fitness communities across the Philippines.

Our Services

Discover the proposed features of MOVEO.

01

GPS Activity Tracking

Track walking and running activities, including distance, time, and pace.

02

Local Event Discovery

Explore fun runs, walkathons, and other fitness events in your area.

03

Event Information

View event dates, venues, fees, deadlines, and organizer details.

04

Official Registration

Access official registration links provided by event organizers.

05

Activity History

Review previously recorded walking and running activities.

06

Event Submission

Allow organizers to submit local fitness events for review.
